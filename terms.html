Terms of Service

The rules of the hunt. By signing in and playing Cryptonx'26, you agree to these terms.

Eligibility

Cryptonx'26 is open to GSV first-year students signing in with their official college Google account. Accounts outside this domain will be signed out automatically.

Gameplay Rules

  • Each level must be solved and submitted individually to progress and earn points.
  • Sharing answers, hints, or solutions with other participants is not allowed.
  • Attempting to exploit, brute-force, or otherwise bypass the intended puzzle mechanics is not allowed.
  • Points and rank on the leaderboard are based solely on levels solved through legitimate play.

Disqualification & Bans

Organizers may flag or ban an account found violating these rules. A banned account loses access to the hunt and may be excluded from the leaderboard. Organizer decisions on disqualification are final.

Leaderboard & Results

The leaderboard updates automatically as points are earned. Organizer accounts are shown separately and are not part of the competitive ranking. Final standings at the end of the hunt window are considered official.

Questions

For questions about these terms, reach out to the organizers listed on the Credits page. See also our Privacy Policy.
